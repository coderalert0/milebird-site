What's free

Automatic drive detection with no monthly limit, adding and editing drives, sorting them as Business or Personal, the IRS rate table, CSV export, backup files, tracking-health alerts and Diagnostics are free, with no account. Drives you've logged are never locked behind a purchase: whether or not you have Milebird Pro, you can see, edit and export every one.

Milebird is a record-keeping tool, not tax advice

Milebird records drives from your iPhone's location and motion sensors and works out distances from them. GPS and sensors can be wrong or unavailable, iOS decides when an app may run, and a drive can be missed, split, joined, mislabeled or measured inaccurately; stretches Milebird had to estimate are marked as estimated. Review your log, correct it where it's wrong, and keep your own records as the tax rules require. Mileage rates in the app are provided for convenience from the published rates of the IRS, the CRA, HMRC and the ATO, and may change; check the rate that applies to you. Whether a drive is deductible or reimbursable depends on your circumstances and your employer's policy. Nothing in Milebird or on this website is tax, legal or financial advice: ask a tax professional.
