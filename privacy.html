Privacy policy

Effective November 11, 2026.

Milebird is an automatic mileage log for iPhone. It's built so your drives stay yours: there's no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising, and Milebird contains no third-party code. Nothing about you or your drives is sent to us.

Location and motion

To log drives automatically, Milebird uses your iPhone's location (with your permission set to Always, so drives are logged while the app is closed) and Motion & Fitness activity (whether you're walking, still or in a vehicle). Between drives the GPS is off and iOS only tells Milebird when you've moved; the GPS runs while Milebird checks a possible drive and while you drive. All of this is processed on your iPhone. Your location never leaves it.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your drives: each drive's start and end times, its route and distance, its stops, the places you name (such as Home or a client), the category and purpose you choose, and the IRS rate applied.
  • Car connection names: while a drive is recorded, the name of the car audio it's connected to over CarPlay or Bluetooth, so Milebird can tell your cars from someone else's.
  • Diagnostics: a log of the app's own events, such as when iOS woke it, how long GPS ran, permission changes and your battery level at the start and end of drives.
  • Settings: your rules, work hours, places, cars, stop settings and mileage rates.

These are stored in Milebird's files on your iPhone and are included in your iPhone's own iCloud or computer backup and in a Quick Start transfer to a new iPhone, as Apple provides for every app's files. Milebird doesn't send them anywhere. Deleting Milebird deletes them from the iPhone.

Maps

A drive's route is shown on a map drawn by Apple Maps (Apple's MapKit). To show the map, iOS loads map images for that area from Apple, as it does for any app with a map; Apple's own privacy policy covers that. Milebird does not send your drives or your route to Apple or anyone else.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Milebird uses them only to tell you when tracking can't work (for example, when Location is turned off) and, with Milebird Pro's free trial, a reminder before the trial ends. They are created on your iPhone.

Files you share

When you export your drives as CSV, save a backup file or share the Diagnostics log, you choose where the file goes (Mail, Messages, AirDrop, Files…). Milebird only creates the file. If you email the Diagnostics log to support, we use it only to answer you.

iCloud sync

Milebird doesn't sync through iCloud today: your drives are on the iPhone that logged them. If iCloud sync is added, it will use your own private iCloud database, which only your Apple Account can read, and this policy will be updated before it ships.

Purchases

Milebird Pro is sold and billed by Apple. Milebird learns only whether you have Pro, never your name or payment details.

What is not collected

No account, no contact details, no identifiers, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no data sold or shared with anyone. Milebird's privacy manifest declares that it collects no data and doesn't track you.

Children

Milebird is a tool for drivers and isn't directed to children. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
