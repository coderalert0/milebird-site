Privacy policy

Effective November 12, 2026.

Milebird is an automatic mileage log for iPhone. It's built so your drives stay yours: there's no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising, and Milebird contains no third-party code. Nothing about you or your drives is sent to us. The only things that leave your iPhone go to Apple, as described below: map images, address lookups and, if you turn it on, your own iCloud.

Location and motion

To log drives automatically, Milebird uses your iPhone's location (with your permission set to Always, so drives are logged while the app is closed) and Motion & Fitness activity (whether you're walking, still or in a vehicle). Between drives the GPS is off and iOS only tells Milebird when you've moved; the GPS runs while Milebird checks a possible drive and while you drive. All of this is processed on your iPhone. Your location never leaves it.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your drives: each drive's start and end times, its route and distance, its stops, the places you name (such as Home or a client) and the addresses of places you haven't named, the category and purpose you choose, the vehicle and client, parking, tolls and notes you add, and the rate applied.
  • Car connection names: while a drive is recorded, the name of the car audio it's connected to over CarPlay or Bluetooth, so Milebird can tell your cars from someone else's.
  • Diagnostics: a log of the app's own events, such as when iOS woke it, how long GPS ran, permission changes and your battery level at the start and end of drives.
  • Settings: your rules, work hours, places, cars and vehicles with their odometer readings, clients and their rates, stop settings, region and mileage rates, reminders, and your accountant's email address if you enter one.

These are stored in Milebird's files on your iPhone and are included in your iPhone's own iCloud or computer backup and in a Quick Start transfer to a new iPhone, as Apple provides for every app's files. Apart from iCloud sync, if you turn it on, Milebird doesn't send them anywhere. Deleting Milebird deletes them from the iPhone.

Maps and addresses

A drive's route is shown on a map drawn by Apple Maps (Apple's MapKit). To show the map, iOS loads map images for that area from Apple, as it does for any app with a map. For the start or end of a drive at a place you haven't named, Milebird asks Apple's geocoder, part of iOS, for the street address: only that point's coordinates are sent, and the address that comes back is stored with the drive on your iPhone. Apple's own privacy policy covers both. Milebird doesn't send your drives or routes to anyone for these.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Milebird uses them only to tell you when tracking can't work (for example, when Location is turned off), for a reminder before a Milebird Pro free trial ends, and, only if you turn them on in Settings › Reminders, an evening reminder to sort drives and a month-end note that your log is ready. They are created on your iPhone. The Live Activity that shows a drive in progress on the Lock Screen, and Siri and Shortcuts actions, also run on your iPhone.

Files you share

When you export your drives as CSV, save a backup file or share the Diagnostics log, you choose where the file goes (Mail, Messages, AirDrop, Files…). Milebird only creates the file. When you import a CSV file or restore a backup, Milebird reads only the file you pick. Email support (in Diagnostics) opens your own mail app with a message that includes the Diagnostics summary and recent log, and Send to my accountant (in Reports) opens it with your log attached; you see each message and decide whether to send it. If you email us, we use what you send only to answer you.

iCloud sync

iCloud sync is part of Milebird Pro and is off until you turn it on (Settings › iCloud). When it's on, your drives and their routes are stored in your own private iCloud database (Apple's CloudKit), so every iPhone signed in to your Apple Account gets them. Only your Apple Account can read that database; we can't, and Milebird has no server of its own. Turning sync off stops it. Apple's iCloud terms and privacy policy cover iCloud storage.

Purchases

Milebird Pro is sold and billed by Apple. Milebird learns only whether you have Pro, never your name or payment details.

What is not collected

No account, no contact details, no identifiers, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no data sold or shared with anyone. Milebird's privacy manifest declares that it collects no data and doesn't track you.

Children

Milebird is a tool for drivers and isn't directed to children. It collects no personal information from anyone.
