Support

A person reads and answers every email, usually within one business day. Write to laddothis at gmail dot com with what happened, your iPhone model and its iOS version. If a drive was missed or looks wrong, attach the Diagnostics log: open the Diagnostics tab, tap Share log and choose Mail. The log holds the app's own events (wake-ups, GPS modes, drives, permissions and battery levels), so we can see what happened without asking you to repeat it.

If drives aren't being logged

Check Milebird's permissions

Open the iPhone's Settings, then Apps › Milebird (on iOS 17, scroll down to Milebird), and check four things: Location is set to Always; Precise Location is on; Motion & Fitness is on; Background App Refresh is on. With Location set to While Using, drives are logged only while Milebird is open, and approximate location can't measure miles. Milebird's Diagnostics tab lists each permission, and a red banner on the Drives tab names anything that's off. Notifications are optional: Milebird only uses them to tell you when tracking can't work.

Low Power Mode is on

Apple says Low Power Mode turns off Background App Refresh, among other things. Milebird keeps logging drives, but iOS may wake it a little later, so the start of a drive can be caught late; the stretch it missed is drawn from where you parked and marked as estimated. Diagnostics notes when Low Power Mode is on. To turn it off: Settings › Battery (on iPhone 15 and later, Settings › Battery › Power Mode).

Don't force-quit Milebird

There's no need to close Milebird: between drives the GPS is off and iOS wakes the app when you move. Swiping it away in the app switcher stops the app, which can cut the GPS off in the middle of a drive and delay the next one until iOS wakes Milebird again. Leave it in the app switcher. If you did swipe it away, open it once.

Tracking is paused

A banner on the Drives tab shows a pause, with a Resume button. A pause for an hour or until tomorrow ends by itself; Until I resume lasts until you tap Resume or turn off Pause tracking in Settings › Tracking.

A drive is missing

Open Diagnostics to see when iOS last woke Milebird and what it saw, and check the permissions above. To add the drive yourself: on the Drives tab, tap + › Add a drive by hand. You can also tap + › Start a drive now before you set off, and End this drive now when you arrive.

A drive was split at a stop, or two drives were joined

Open the drive: Split cuts it at any of its stops, and Join with the drive before puts two back together. To change what happens next time, use Settings › Tracking: End a drive after stopping (1 to 15 minutes, 4 by default) and Join drives with stops under (for deliveries and site visits; each stop is still listed).

A train ride or someone else's car was logged

Tap Not a drive. It's kept on record but left out of your totals. Rides that look like a train or a bus, and rides in a car that isn't one of yours, are flagged for you to check; mark your own cars in Settings › My cars so Milebird can tell them apart by CarPlay or Bluetooth.

Your drives and files

How do I export my drives?

Settings › Your data › Export all drives (CSV). The file has one row per drive: date, start and end times, from, to, miles, category, purpose, vehicle, rate (cents a mile) and value. Send it with Mail or AirDrop, or save it to Files. It's free, any time, for every drive.

How do I move to a new iPhone?

Your drives are part of your iPhone's own backup, so restoring the new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup, or moving with Quick Start, brings them along. You can also save a file yourself: Settings › Your data › Save a backup file, then on the new iPhone Settings › Your data › Restore from a backup file. Restoring merges drives without making duplicates.

How do I delete a drive?

Swipe the drive, or open it and tap Delete drive. It's removed from your log and your totals. To keep it on record but out of your totals, choose Not a drive instead.

Which mileage rate is used?

The IRS rate in force on each drive's date, from the table in Settings › Mileage rates › IRS standard mileage rates. For 2026: 72.5 cents a mile for business through June 30, and 76 cents from July 1. You can edit the table or add a rate period.

Milebird Pro and billing

How do I cancel?

Milebird Pro is billed by Apple. On your iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Milebird, then Cancel Subscription, at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Cancel at least 24 hours before a free trial ends and you won't be charged.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles refunds for App Store purchases. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

What do I keep if Pro ends?

Every drive. You can still see, sort, edit and export all of them. Rules stop sorting new drives, but the choices they already made stay.

I bought Pro. How do I get it on a new iPhone?

Purchases belong to your Apple Account. Sign in with the same Apple Account and use Restore Purchases in Milebird.
