## What changed at MileIQ

MileIQ's Unlimited plan went up in 2026. PricingSaaS, which tracks companies' pricing pages, recorded the increase in its second-quarter 2026 snapshot (detected July 3, 2026):

| MileIQ Unlimited | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Billed monthly | $8.99 a month | $13.99 a month |
| Billed yearly | $7.50 a month ($89.99 a year) | $11.66 a month ($139.99 a year) |

On November 11, 2026, MileIQ's pricing page showed the same prices, and its US App Store listing offered in-app purchases at $13.99 (monthly) and $139.99 (yearly). The free plan is 40 drives a month, with automatic tracking and reports. On the App Store, MileIQ is published by Bending Spoons US Inc.

## What to compare besides the price

- **The free plan's limit.** A monthly cap on drives or trips, or none.
- **What happens to drives you've logged** if you stop paying: can you still see and export them?
- **Export.** Which formats (CSV, PDF), and whether you can export any time or only on a paid plan.
- **The mileage rate.** The IRS raised the business rate from 72.5 to 76 cents a mile for miles driven from July 1, 2026; check that the app values each drive at the rate for its date.
- **Accounts and data.** Whether you need an account, and where your drives are stored.
- **Trials and cancelling.** The trial's length, whether it needs a card, whether you get a reminder before it converts, and how you cancel.
- **Support.** How you reach a person when a drive goes missing.

## Moving your records

1. **Export first.** Before you cancel any mileage app, download the reports or files for every year you may need. The IRS generally expects records that support a deduction to be kept for 3 years after you file ([IRS Publication 463](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p463)).
2. **Start the new app before you cancel the old one.** Running both for a week shows you whether the new one catches your drives.
3. **Cancel where you pay.** If you subscribed through Apple, cancel in Settings › your name › Subscriptions. If you paid on the company's website, cancel there.
